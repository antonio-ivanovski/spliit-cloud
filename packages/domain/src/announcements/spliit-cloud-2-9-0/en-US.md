Spliit Cloud 2.9.0 adds built-in Tricount and Settle Up imports, so you can bring your existing groups with you. Details are in the [v2.9.0](https://github.com/antonio-ivanovski/spliit-cloud/releases/tag/v2.9.0) notes.

## From Tricount, two ways in

Paste a Tricount share link and the group is fetched server-side — no GDPR request needed — or drop a GDPR CSV export. Dumps holding several groups get a picker with per-group participant and expense counts, and each group flows through mapping and currency conversion in turn.

## From Settle Up, one CSV

Email the group history CSV to yourself from Settle Up, then drop it onto the Settle Up tab. Settlements and income come across, categories map to Spliit categories, and amounts convert to your destination currency with historic rates at confirm time.

## Stuck finding the export?

Every importer input now carries its own step-by-step guide that answers the exact question you have at that input — where the link lives, or where the file comes from.
