Spliit Cloud 2.10.0 makes inviting people way easier: share one link with the whole group, start anonymously, and keep your invite through sign-in. Details are in the [v2.10.0](https://github.com/antonio-ivanovski/spliit-cloud/releases/tag/v2.10.0) notes.

## One link for several people

Spin up one shared invite for the family chat, the trip, or the community. Set a join limit and expiry, or kick off with unlimited joins for seven days. Re-copy the link from pending invites, and creators or admins can tweak its limits, rotate it, or pull it when you're done.

## Anonymous access first

Anonymous access is now the first option on the getting-started screen, with clearer wording and branded social sign-in buttons.

## Invites survive sign-in and setup

Group viewing and invite links hold onto their access tokens through sign-in, signup, profile setup, and recovery — even emailed callbacks opened on another device. Hit “Sign in to accept” and you land straight on the group's expenses after joining.
