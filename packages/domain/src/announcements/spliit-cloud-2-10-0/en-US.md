Spliit Cloud 2.10.0 makes inviting people easier: share one link with your whole group, start anonymously, and keep your invitation through sign-in. Details are in the [v2.10.0](https://github.com/antonio-ivanovski/spliit-cloud/releases/tag/v2.10.0) notes.

## One link for several people

Create a shared invitation for a family chat, trip, or community. Choose a join limit and expiry, or start with unlimited joins for seven days. Copy the link again from pending invitations, and creators or admins can edit its limits, rotate it, or revoke it when done.

## Anonymous access first

Anonymous access is now the first option on the getting-started screen, with clearer wording and branded social sign-in buttons.

## Invites survive sign-in and setup

Group viewing and invitation links keep their access tokens through sign-in, signup, profile setup, and recovery, including emailed callbacks opened on another device. Choosing “Sign in to accept” takes you straight to the group's expenses after joining.
