### 🚀 Features

- Halloween appearance: Spliit dresses up with haunted scenery and seasonal
  colors from October 30 through November 1, with an appearance switch that
  remembers your opt-out in this browser (`TBD` by @TBD)
- Personal expenses: an account-owned spending ledger with monthly budgets,
  linked group/friend expenses at your share, CSV bank import, smart
  categorization, and a reviewed batch-categorization run (`TBD` by @TBD)
- Personal receipts: attach images/PDFs to native personal entries (up to 10
  per entry), with S3 cleanup on delete (`TBD` by @TBD)
- Personal planned transactions: schedule repeating expenses or income
  (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly) from the new Planned tab; future occurrences
  show under Upcoming and materialize into transactions automatically, with
  pause/resume (no backfill), skip-next, cancel, and one-off edits that detach
  from the plan (`TBD` by @TBD)
- Personal backup and nightly repair: account exports now include an optional
  personal slice (`personal/manifest.json` + `personal/transactions.json`,
  provenance preserved, attachments under `personal/documents/`), and a daily
  reconcile job repairs missed group links with bounded, no-op-safe sync
  (`TBD` by @TBD)
