### Bulk-categorize existing expenses from Tools

Pick Local matching or the System One decision model and categorize a whole group at once. Confirm a few calibration samples, review suggestions in a fast virtualized table with match strength and confidence, rerun remaining General or uncertain expenses using your corrections, and save the selected categories atomically. Progress tracks processed expenses across calibration, refinement, and reruns; runs are shared between group admins with safe retries and conflict recovery; large groups up to 10,000 expenses are supported.
