Welcome to `v2.3.3`! A bug-fix patch — editing an expense that includes a removed member no longer fails, crypto conversions report honest rate dates, and iOS stops auto-zooming when you focus a field.

## Highlights

- Editing expenses with removed members works again
- Honest crypto conversion dates
- No more iOS auto-zoom on focus

### Editing expenses with removed members works again

If a member was removed from a group, editing an old expense they were part of used to fail with "Invalid Participant ID" — even a no-op resave. Stored participant IDs are now grandfathered on every leg (including itemized splits), so the edit form round-trips them cleanly and removed members stay on the expenses they were part of.

### Honest crypto conversion dates

Bridged rates (e.g. DOGE→MKD via EUR) now report the oldest leg's date so the stale-rate warning is accurate, reversed quotes are marked as inverted in the rate sources, future dates consistently resolve at today's rate on the server, and invalid dates return a proper validation error instead of a provider failure.

### No more iOS auto-zoom on focus

iOS Safari auto-zooms when a focused field is under 16px. Shared inputs, textareas, selects, search, command palette, and OTP fields now stay at 16px with a coarse-pointer safety net, so focusing a field no longer zooms the page — while user pinch-zoom still works.
