Welcome to `v2.10.1`: a patch that makes PWA updates apply reliably and polishes the installed-app appearance.

## What's Changed

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- Installed apps have better-proportioned home-screen icons, clearer monochrome notification icons, and launch and system-bar colors that follow the app surface. Android startup also shows branding while checking for package updates. ([`5958ead7`](https://github.com/antonio-ivanovski/spliit-cloud/commit/5958ead73b4e3619b2ff2020221986a90b853284) by @antonio-ivanovski)

- PWA updates are discovered promptly and apply automatically when safe. Expense drafts survive the update reload with a five-second notice, while other unfinished work keeps the update paused. ([`be1911c1`](https://github.com/antonio-ivanovski/spliit-cloud/commit/be1911c1e2a3d73fd1c6979070c30d1ed2bad415) by @antonio-ivanovski)
