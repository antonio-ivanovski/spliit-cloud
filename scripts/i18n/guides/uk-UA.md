# uk-UA translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Ukrainian rules.

- Use standard Ukrainian in Cyrillic with a friendly neutral UI voice. Do not substitute Russian vocabulary, grammar, or orthography.
- Decline nouns, names, and adjectives in the correct case and gender; rephrase when an unnecessary gender assumption can be avoided.
- Respect Ukrainian plural categories and count-dependent forms. Keep `{count}` in each required form and do not copy Russian plural wording.
- Preserve Ukrainian apostrophes, diacritics, quotation marks, and punctuation.
- Use stable Ukrainian product terminology for expenses, shares, balances, and settlements, with no change to numeric meaning.
