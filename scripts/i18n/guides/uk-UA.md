# uk-UA translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Ukrainian rules.

- Use standard Ukrainian in Cyrillic with a friendly neutral UI voice. Do not substitute Russian vocabulary, grammar, or orthography. Brand is `Spliit Cloud` (never `Імпорт Spliit Cloud` in source pickers); product typo `Splitit` is wrong.
- Decline nouns, names, and adjectives in the correct case and gender; rephrase when an unnecessary gender assumption can be avoided.
- Respect Ukrainian plural categories and count-dependent forms. Keep `{count}` in each required form and do not copy Russian plural wording.
- Preserve Ukrainian apostrophes, diacritics, quotation marks, and punctuation.
- Use stable Ukrainian product terminology for expenses, shares, balances, and settlements, with no change to numeric meaning. Itemized split is `Позиціями` (`Частка кожного учасника розраховується з окремих позицій витрати.`). Fixed-rate import uses a single exchange rate per currency pair from Frankfurter (not "Менш точно/нульовим"). Income placeholder is `Зарплата або повернення` (repayment, not settlement; copy of the expense example is wrong).
- Exclusive settlement term: _розрахунок_ (noun) and _розрахуватися_ (verb). Never _відшкодування_ or _компенсація_ for this concept. For unsettled states use `нерозраховані баланси`/`нерозрахованими`/`розраховано` and for CTAs `Розрахуватися`/`Розрахунковий платіж`, not bureaucratic `неврегульовані` or debt wording `погасити`/`заборгованість`.
