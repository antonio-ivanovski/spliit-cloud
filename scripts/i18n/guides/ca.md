# ca translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Catalan rules.

- Use standard central Catalan, not Spanish with Catalan spelling. Avoid Spanish calques and regionally marked Valencian/Balearic wording unless the existing locale establishes it. Prefer standard forms: _això_ (not _açò_), _tingui_ (not _tinga_), _quantitats_ (not _quantitas_), and unaccented futures _mostraran_/_es mostrarà_ (not _mostraràn_).
- Keep a consistent informal singular `tu` register (e.g. _Tria_, _Afegeix_, _Selecciona_, _Cerca_) across imperatives and placeholders; use possessive _teu/teves_ rather than formal _vostre/vostès_ except where the surrounding flow already establishes a formal account-ownership context. Do not switch between `tu` and `vosaltres` within one related flow.
- Preserve Catalan accents, apostrophes, and elisions (`l'`, `d'`) and use Catalan punctuation spacing naturally. Keep the ellipsis character `…` where English uses `…` (e.g. _Creant…_, _Desant…_, _Cerca una despesa…_).
- Use Catalan terms for groups, members, expenses, shares, and settlements consistently across the bundle. Balance is _saldo_ (financial position) / _saldos_; avoid _balanç_ outside the top-level navigation title _Balanç_, and never use _balanços_ for monetary balances. Unified participant wording is _participant_ (generic masculine) — never feminine _una participant_/_les participants_ for the generic role.
- Exclusive settlement term: _liquidació_ (noun) and _liquidar_ (verb). Never _reemborsament_. For already-settled states, prefer _liquidat/liquidats_ (not _saldat/saldats_) to stay aligned with the exclusive verb.
- Respect grammatical gender and number, but prefer neutral rephrasing when a label does not need to name a person. Use singular _Afegeix un participant_ for add-action labels (not plural _Afegir participants_) and _Selecciona un participant…_ for pickers.
