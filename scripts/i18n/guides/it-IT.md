# it-IT translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Italian (Italy) rules.

- Use standard Italian for Italy with a friendly neutral `tu`/impersonal UI voice; avoid switching to formal `Lei` without a clear reason.
- Preserve accents, apostrophes, elision, and correct gender/number agreement. Rephrase when a short label needlessly assigns gender.
- Use natural Italian word order and established product vocabulary instead of literal English calques.
- Exclusive settlement term: *liquidazione* (noun) and *liquidare* (verb). Never *rimborso*. Do not use *saldo* for settlement; *saldo* already means balance.
- Keep action labels concise, usually with an infinitive or a consistent imperative style across related controls.
- Respect Italian punctuation and spacing while leaving tags, URLs, and placeholders unchanged.
