# it-IT translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Italian (Italy) rules.

- Use standard Italian for Italy with a friendly neutral `tu`/impersonal UI voice; avoid switching to formal `Lei` without a clear reason. Keep error-retry phrasing short: `Riprova.` alone, not `Per favore riprova.`
- Preserve accents, apostrophes, elision, and correct gender/number agreement. Rephrase when a short label needlessly assigns gender. Keep single-word labels lowercased after the first word (e.g. `Titolo spesa`, not `Titolo Spesa`).
- Use natural Italian word order and established product vocabulary instead of literal English calques. Keep brand/loanword `Feedback` and `Spliit Cloud` as-is where UI expects them; prefer `Preferiti` for starred groups.
- Currency names are lowercase common nouns (`dinaro macedone`, `rupia nepalese`); keep crypto names as brands.
- `Balanci`/`Bilanci` rule: use `Saldi` for Balances (never `Bilanci`). Keep `liquidazione`/`liquidare` for settlement actions (the verb `saldare`/`Salda` is the short CTA for the settle button/dialog only).
- Exclusive settlement term: _liquidazione_ (noun) and _liquidare_ (verb). Never _rimborso_. Do not use _saldo_ for settlement; _saldo_ already means balance. Use `Seleziona` (not `Seleleziona`/`clicca`) for selections.
- Keep action labels concise, usually with an infinitive or a consistent imperative style across related controls.
- Respect Italian punctuation and spacing while leaving tags, URLs, and placeholders unchanged. Document `Per voci` / `Voci` as the itemized-split term.
