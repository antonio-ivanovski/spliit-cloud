# es translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Spanish rules.

- Use neutral international Spanish. Avoid strongly regional voseo, vosotros-only wording, or country-specific slang.
- Keep a friendly concise `tú`/impersonal UI voice and use opening question/exclamation marks where Spanish punctuation requires them.
- Preserve accents and natural gender/number agreement. Rephrase when a short label would otherwise force unnecessary gender.
- Do not translate as English word order; use established Spanish terms consistently for members, groups, expenses, shares, and settlements.
- Exclusive settlement term: *liquidación* (noun) and *liquidar* (verb). Never *reembolso* or *reembolsar*.
- Keep decimal, currency, and thousands formatting consistent with the app and do not change numeric meaning.
