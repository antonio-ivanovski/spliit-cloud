# es translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Spanish rules.

- Use neutral international Spanish. Avoid strongly regional voseo, vosotros-only wording, or country-specific slang.
- Keep a friendly concise `tú`/impersonal UI voice throughout; never mix `tú` and `usted` in the same flow. Use `tú` imperatives (`Selecciona`, `Introduce`, `Vuelve`) — never `Seleccione`/`Introduzca`/`Vuelva`. Use opening `¿`/`¡` paired with `?`/`!` where Spanish punctuation requires them.
- Preserve accents (`Aquí`, `aún`, `Categoría`, `Título`, `Sí`, `Música`, `Último`, etc.) and natural gender/number agreement. Rephrase when a short label would otherwise force unnecessary gender.
- Do not translate as English word order; use established Spanish terms consistently for members, groups, expenses, shares, and settlements (`Por artículos` for Itemized).
- Exclusive settlement term: _liquidación_ (noun) and _liquidar_ (verb). Never _reembolso_ or _reembolsar_.
- Keep decimal, currency, and thousands formatting consistent with the app and do not change numeric meaning. Keep every plural category (`_zero`/`_one`/`_two`/`_few`/`_many`/`_other`) even when Spanish only distinguishes `_one`/`_other` — alias to the natural form.
