# sv-SE translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Swedish (Sweden) rules.

- Use standard Swedish for Sweden with an informal `du`/impersonal UI voice; avoid honorific distance.
- Use natural Swedish compounds and word order rather than English-shaped phrases. Keep compounds readable in narrow controls.
- Swedish has common/neuter noun gender (`en`/`ett`); maintain agreement but avoid gendered language about users where possible.
- Preserve Swedish diacritics and punctuation, and do not capitalize every word in a label.
- Keep terminology stable for expenses, shares, balances, and settlements and preserve exact numeric meaning. Import step labels: `Mål` for Destination (never untranslated `Destination`). Confidence is `Hög`/`Mellan`/`Låg` (never English `Medium`). Category `Life/Donation` is `Donation` as accepted loanword.
- Exclusive settlement term: _reglering_ (noun) and _reglera_ (verb). Never _återbetalning_ or _ersättning_ for this concept. Income placeholder `Lön eller återbetalning` is repayment, not settlement, and is the only allowed _återbetalning_; anonymous-account `ersättning`/`ersättningslänk` is a replacement link, not a settlement.
