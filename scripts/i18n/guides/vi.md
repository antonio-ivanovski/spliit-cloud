# vi translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Vietnamese rules.

- Use standard Vietnamese with a friendly, neutral UI voice. Avoid region-specific slang unless the surrounding bundle clearly establishes a region.
- Pronouns encode relationship and age; omit them when context allows, and do not guess kinship terms for an unknown user.
- Preserve Vietnamese tone marks and natural word order. Use classifiers and measure words only when Vietnamese requires them.
- Vietnamese often expresses plurality through context or a number; still provide natural text for every required plural key.
- Keep labels informative — avoid severe truncation that drops meaning. Preserve the full intent of import, balance, and settlement descriptions.
- Keep labels concise, avoid English calques, and preserve exact numeric and currency meaning. Brand is `Spliit Cloud` (never `Nhập Spliit Cloud` in source pickers). Itemized split is `Theo mục` (`Tính phần chia của mỗi người dựa trên từng mục trong chi phí.`). Fixed-rate import uses a single exchange rate per currency pair from Frankfurter (not `Dùng một tỷ giá.`).
- Exclusive settlement term: _tất toán_ (noun and verb). Never _hoàn trả_, _hoàn tiền_, or refund cognates. Use `tất toán` for settlement (not generic `thanh toán`); `thanh toán` remains for ordinary payments only.
