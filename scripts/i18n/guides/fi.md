# fi translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Finnish rules.

- Use standard contemporary Finnish with a neutral, approachable UI voice; avoid overly formal bureaucratic wording.
- Finnish is case-heavy and agglutinative. Use the correct case ending and natural word order rather than translating English prepositions literally.
- Build readable Finnish compounds and keep labels concise. Do not insert spaces inside a word that should be one compound.
- Finnish has no grammatical gender; do not introduce gendered pronouns or nouns unnecessarily.
- Preserve Finnish diacritics and natural punctuation, and fill plural forms as complete natural phrases.
- Exclusive settlement term: _tasaus_ (noun) and _tasata_ (verb). Never _korvaus_ or _hyvitys_ for this concept.
