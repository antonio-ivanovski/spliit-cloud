# pt translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these European Portuguese rules.

- Treat the generic `pt` locale as European Portuguese and keep it distinct from `pt-BR`.
- Use a natural Portugal register, generally impersonal or consistent `tu` phrasing; avoid Brazilian `você` and Brazilian-only vocabulary (`Somente` → `Apenas`/`Só`, `você` → `tu`/`—`).
- Preserve European spelling, accents, contractions, and punctuation. Prefer Portugal terms for files, phones, groups, expenses, and balances where the distinction matters (`ecrã`/`tela`, `autocarro`/`ônibus`, `telemóvel`/`celular`, `partilhar`/`compartilhar`, `ficheiro`/`ficheiro` vs BR `arquivo`).
- Match gender, number, and article agreement carefully; `ligação` is feminine (`a ligação`, `da ligação`, `à ligação`) and `conta anónima` agreement; rephrase short labels when that is more natural and inclusive.
- Keep action labels concise and do not change numeric or currency meaning.
- Prefer Portugal UI vocabulary: `Definições` for Settings (not `Configurações`), `personalizar` (not `customizar`), `navegador` (not `browser`), `ligação` for invite/sign-in links (not `link`), product name `Spliit` (not `Split`).
- Exclusive settlement term: _liquidação_ (noun) and _liquidar_ (verb). Never _reembolso_.
