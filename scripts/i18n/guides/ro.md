# ro translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these Romanian rules.

- Use standard Romanian with a friendly neutral UI voice; avoid regionally marked or Moldovan/Russian calques. Prefer the informal singular imperative (`Vezi`, `Decontează`, `Preferă`, `Spune-ne`) over formal plural (`Preferați`, `Copiați`, `Traduceți`).
- Preserve Romanian diacritics (`ă`, `â`, `î`, `ș`, `ț`) and correct article, gender, and case agreement.
- Use natural Romanian word order and established product terms instead of mirroring English syntax.
- Respect Romanian plural behavior and write every required form as a complete natural phrase containing `{count}` where required.
- Keep labels concise and terminology stable across members, groups, expenses, shares, and settlements. Friend-ledger settle-up is `Decontează unu-la-unu`; archive balances are `Solduri nedecontate` (not _nereglate_).
- Product name is always `Spliit Cloud` (never _Split Cloud_). Settlement reporting is `decontat` (not _achitat_); conditional settlement note uses `nedecontate`.
- Exclusive settlement term: _decontare_ (noun) and _deconta_ (verb). Never _rambursare_, _regularizare_, _achitare/neachitat_, or _compensație_ for this concept. Exchange-rate phrasing uses `curs de schimb` / `curs de conversie` (Frankfurter) — never the opposite `mai puțin precis` framing.
