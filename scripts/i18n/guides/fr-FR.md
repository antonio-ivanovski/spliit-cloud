# fr-FR translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these French (France) rules.

- Use neutral metropolitan French for France. Avoid Canadian, Belgian, or Swiss regionalisms unless the existing bundle already uses them.
- Keep a friendly but respectful UI voice; use `vous` or impersonal phrasing consistently and do not switch to casual `tu` without context. Example: `Vérifiez votre boîte de réception…` not `Vérifie ta boîte…`.
- Preserve accents, apostrophes, elisions, and French spacing conventions around punctuation such as `:`, `;`, `!`, and `?`. Always use the single-character ellipsis `…` (U+2026), never `...`.
- Prefer established French product terminology over literal English calques, especially for expenses, shares, balances, and settlements. Use `Soldes` for the top-level **Balances** navigation/heading (never `Équilibres`) and `Enregistrer` / `Enregistrement…` for **Save** / **Saving…** (never `Sauvegarder` / `Sauvegarde…`).
- Exclusive settlement term: _règlement_ (noun) and _régler_ (verb). Never _remboursement_, _rembourser_, or refund cognates.
- Currency names: `Livre turque` for TRY (never `Lire turque`).
- Handle gender and agreement carefully; rephrase short labels when that produces a more natural and inclusive result.
