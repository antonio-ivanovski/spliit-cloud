# de-DE translation guide

Apply `default.md` first, then these German (Germany) rules.

- Use standard German for Germany and a friendly `du`/impersonal UI voice; do not switch unpredictably to `Sie`. Imperatives use `Gib`, `Wähle`, `Erstelle`, `Überprüfe` etc., never `Geben Sie`/`Wählen Sie`. Third-person `Sie` at sentence start that refers to a feminine noun (`die Gruppe`, `die KI`) or plural `sie` (= they) is not formal and is correct.
- Capitalize nouns correctly and keep compound terms readable. Prefer a short natural compound over a long English-shaped phrase. Brand compounds hyphenate in running text (`Spliit-Cloud-Sicherung`), otherwise keep `Spliit Cloud` as the product name.
- Use Germany terminology rather than Swiss or Austrian variants unless the existing bundle establishes another term. Exchange/conversion rate is `Wechselkurs`/`Kurs`/`Umrechnungskurs`, never `Zinssatz` (interest rate). Itemized split is `Nach Artikeln` (`Der Anteil jeder Person wird aus den einzelnen Artikeln der Ausgabe berechnet.`).
- Preserve grammatical gender and case, but rephrase when that avoids awkward gendered references in a short UI label.
- Keep numbers, currency, and punctuation readable in German; never alter the app's numeric value or placeholder syntax.
- Exclusive settlement term: _Ausgleich_ (noun) and _ausgleichen_ (verb). Never _Erstattung_ or _Rückerstattung_. Income placeholder `Gehalt oder Rückzahlung` uses `Rückzahlung` (repayment) and is not a settlement.
