# Spliit Cloud

Short description: Split shared expenses with friends and family. Free and open source.

Track shared expenses, see who owes whom, and organize trips, households, and everyday spending together.

- Create groups and split expenses in different ways.
- Track balances and record settlements.
- Attach and share receipts.
- Browse previously synchronized expenses offline and queue supported new expenses.
- Import and export your data.
- Use optional notifications and receipt extraction when configured.

Spliit Cloud is free, without ads. This Android application connects to https://spliit.cloud using your browser. An up-to-date compatible browser is required; an internet connection is required for first setup, sign-in, synchronization, and online features.

Recording a settlement does not transfer money. Spliit Cloud is an expense-tracking tool, not a bank, wallet, or payment service.

Support: https://spliit.cloud/support
Privacy: https://spliit.cloud/privacy
Delete your account: https://spliit.cloud/delete-account

- Data safety: inventory account/email/avatar/session identifiers, shared financial/expense records, documents/photos, optional audio/transcripts, notification subscriptions, IP/security logs, exports, and enabled AI/OCR providers. Apply Google's service-provider and ephemeral-processing rules to actual deployment behavior.
- AI: extraction improves existing productivity features; check current policy applicability and any enabled free-form generation before submission.
- Audience/content rating: answer based on actual content and intended audience; the app is not designed specifically for children.
- Deletion: verify the background worker executes requests after the 48-hour grace period and that privacy/support email is monitored.
- Contributions: Android presentation omits payment options until their terms and Play compatibility have been reviewed.
