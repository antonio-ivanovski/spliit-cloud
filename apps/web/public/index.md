# Share expenses with friends & family

Spliit Cloud is a free, open-source, community-maintained app for tracking shared expenses and settling up. Sign in once to protect your data and keep your expense history available.

## Features

- **Groups + sync**: Create groups for trips, events, gifts, and keep them synced across devices.
- **Expenses + receipts**: Add expenses, attach receipt images, and create daily, weekly, or monthly recurring costs.
- **Advanced split**: Split by percentage, shares, exact amount, or a simple equal share.
- **Balances**: See who paid what and optimize money transfers between participants.
- **Categories**: Assign categories so shared spending stays easy to scan.
- **Accounts**: Sign in once to protect your data and keep your expense history available.

## Get started

- Open the app: https://spliit.cloud/
- Create a group: https://spliit.cloud/groups/create
- Import a group from another service: https://spliit.cloud/groups/import
